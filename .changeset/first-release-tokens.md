First release. Three eras to start with grew to six: Terminal / CRT, Classic Windows, Frutiger Aero, Flat / Metro, Neumorphism and Neubrutalism. Five palettes: Dracula, Nord, Solarized Light, Catppuccin Mocha and One Dark Pro, each with its source licence recorded. Colours an era needs beyond the semantic vocabulary are derived in OKLCH per pairing, and every pairing is audited for WCAG 2.1 AA contrast.
